Modified:
- The recognition model's output layer is pruned to its Latin, digit and
  punctuation classes (535 of them, including the CTC blank and the space).
- Both models are converted from ONNX to TensorFlow Lite: the detector with a
  fixed 960 x 960 input, the recognizer with a variable input width.
- Weights are stored as float16.
The text post-processing in this app ports PaddleOCR's DB box extraction and
CTC decoding.
