Cleanleaf is a document scanner that keeps everything on your phone.

- Scan pages with the camera and adjust the corners before saving.
- Edits never touch the original photo, so you can change them later.
- Works offline. There is no account, no sign-in and no analytics.
- Documents stay in the app until you export or share them.

English and Bangla text recognition (OCR) is planned for a later release.
